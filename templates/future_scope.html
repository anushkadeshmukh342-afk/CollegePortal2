Competitive Exams & Future Scope

Prepare for your future with competitive exams and career opportunities

GATE – Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering

🔹 Purpose: For M.Tech, M.E., and PhD admissions; also used for PSU recruitment.

🔹 Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in Engineering/Technology/Architecture or Master's in Science/Arts.

🔹 Pattern: 3-hour CBT, 65 questions (MCQ, MSQ, NAT).

🔹 Conducted by: IITs / IISc.

Official Website
MCAT – Medical College Admission Test

🔹 Purpose: For admission to medical colleges in the USA, Canada, and a few other countries.

🔹 Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in biological or physical sciences; pre-med students.

🔹 Pattern: 7.5-hour computer-based test covering Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Psychology & Critical Reasoning.

🔹 Note: Not conducted in India; nearest centers are in Singapore, Malaysia, and Qatar.

Official Website
GRE – Graduate Record Examination

🔹 Purpose: For admission into global universities (MS, MBA, PhD programs).

🔹 Eligibility: Bachelor's degree (any field). No age limit.

🔹 Pattern: Computer-based test; Verbal, Quantitative & Analytical Writing sections.

🔹 Accepted in: 160+ countries worldwide.

Official Website
CAT – Common Admission Test

🔹 Purpose: For admission into top MBA colleges like IIMs and other B-schools.

🔹 Eligibility: Bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks (45% for SC/ST).

🔹 Pattern: 2-hour computer-based test with sections on VARC, DILR, and QA.

🔹 Mode: Conducted once a year by IIMs.

Official Website
UPSC – Civil Services Examination (CSE)

🔹 Purpose: To recruit for IAS, IPS, IFS, and other central services.

🔹 Eligibility: Graduate degree from any recognized university; age 21–32 years (general category).

🔹 Pattern:

  • Prelims: Objective (General Studies & CSAT)
  • Mains: Descriptive (9 papers)
  • Interview: Personality Test

🔹 Conducted by: Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), Govt. of India.

Official Website
